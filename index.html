Hi! I’m Sarah-Grace, a UBC Engineering student who loves to build things that maybe shouldn’t exist. Outside of school, my hobbies include, well, more engineering, figure skating, hiking, biking, skiing, and flying planes. I used to be a competitive figure skater, violinist, and pianist. I'm also a rare plant collector!
