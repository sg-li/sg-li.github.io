A LITTLE ABOUT ME

Hi, I'm Sarah-Grace!

Hi! I’m Sarah-Grace, a UBC Engineering student who loves to build things that maybe shouldn’t exist. I am always available to chat! Please do not hesitate to reach out if you would like to connect!
